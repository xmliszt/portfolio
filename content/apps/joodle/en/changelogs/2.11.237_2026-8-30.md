**Say hello to Joodle 2.11 — trace a photo, erase a stroke ✨**

### Auto Trace

Point Joodle at any photo and it sketches the outline for you — a head start you finish by hand. Tap the auto trace button in the camera to trace the shot you've framed, or long-press it to pick Simple, Balanced or Detailed for a single trace. Find it under Settings > General > Auto Trace, where you can set the detail level a tap uses. Auto Trace is a Joodle Pro feature.

### Eraser

The trash button now shares a pill with a new eraser. Switch it on and drag across your doodle to lift whole strokes as you go — a circle shows exactly what your finger will erase, and the whole drag undoes in one step.
