**Say hello to Joodle 2.9 — more than one doodle a day ✨**

### More Than One Doodle a Day

Some days hold more than one moment worth drawing. Now they can hold more than one doodle. Keep up to three doodles on a single day, swipe between them right in the day view, and watch your calendar gently cycle through each day's doodles. Every day has room for whatever the day inspires.
