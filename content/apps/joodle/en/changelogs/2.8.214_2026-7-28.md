**Say hello to Joodle 2.8 — line up your reference photo exactly where you want it**

### Adjust Your Reference

Get your tracing reference sitting just right before you draw. Drag to move it around, zoom in on the details, and rotate it however you like to get the best angle to trace your doodle.

### Improvements & Bugfixes

- Added a second camera zoom control you can pull from the opposite screen edge.
- Fixed a stray tap outside the canvas dismissing your in-progress doodle.
- Fixed Pro preview art rendering invisibly in Light appearance.
- Fixed release notes and offers occasionally not showing after an update.
